# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nubit projects a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers via a private GitHub advisory or by contacting the organization owners through GitHub. Maintainers will review and take action they deem appropriate, including warning or banning.

This policy applies in all Nubit spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official Nubit channel.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) version 2.1.
